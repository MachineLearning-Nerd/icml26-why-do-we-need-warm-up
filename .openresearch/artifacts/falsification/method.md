# Method

The route uses exact constant functions to remove numerical uncertainty from Proposition B.2,
checks the paper formula independently with `fractions.Fraction` and SymPy substitution, and
uses the corrected shift-invariant constant as a negative control. It separately audits the stated
Theorem 4.3 domain on an exact quadratic and demonstrates that the historical finite-ray slope
criterion can accept a globally non-`(H0,H1)`-smooth function.
