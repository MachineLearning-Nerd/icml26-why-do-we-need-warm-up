The command exits nonzero if a symbolic certificate, historical regression, balancedness audit,
independent Hessian calibration, affine-envelope check, or predeclared adaptive-GD target fails.

Finite dimension sweeps are corroboration, not universal proofs. The universal component is the
independently reconstructed symbolic/structural certificate. The published Proposition B.2
constant defect and Theorem 4.3 domain omission are reported rather than hidden.
